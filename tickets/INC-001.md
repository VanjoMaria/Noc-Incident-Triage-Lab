# INC-001: Trunk Misconfiguration Isolating Sales VLAN

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Ticket #** | INC-001 |
| **Severity** | Sev2 — degraded, single VLAN isolated, no data loss |
| **Time Detected** | 9:00 am |
| **Symptoms Reported** | Sales VLAN (VLAN 10, 12.4.2.0/26) unable to reach its default gateway (12.4.2.62) or any other VLAN. IT and Finance VLANs unaffected. |
| **Diagnostic Steps Taken** | 1. `show ip interface brief` on the multilayer switch — confirmed VLAN 10 SVI (12.4.2.62) was up/up, ruling out an SVI configuration issue.<br>2. `show interfaces trunk` on the multilayer switch — revealed VLAN 10 was **not** listed under the allowed VLANs on the trunk port connecting to the access switch.<br>3. `show vlan brief` on the access switch — confirmed Sales-facing ports were correctly assigned to VLAN 10, ruling out an access-port misconfiguration. |
| **Root Cause** | The trunk port on the multilayer switch was configured with `switchport trunk allowed vlan 20,30` — VLAN 10 was omitted during initial trunk setup, silently isolating all Sales VLAN traffic from the rest of the network. |
| **Resolution** | Corrected the trunk configuration to `switchport trunk allowed vlan 10,20,30` on both ends of the trunk link. Verified with successful ping tests from a Sales VLAN host to its gateway (12.4.2.62) and to a host in the IT VLAN. |
| **Time to Resolve** | 15min |
| **Prevention / Recommendation** | Standardize a trunk-provisioning checklist that lists all active VLANs explicitly at setup time, rather than configuring them incrementally as VLANs are added — reduces the risk of a VLAN being silently omitted. |
